How this works

  1. Fill in the form with check-up style numbers.
  2. The app adds BMI (and pulse pressure if the saved model needs it).
  3. The trained pipeline scales the numbers and predicts 0 or 1.
  4. You see a probability bar — not a doctor’s diagnosis.
